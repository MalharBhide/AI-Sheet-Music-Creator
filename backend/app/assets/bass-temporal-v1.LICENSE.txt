Training recipe and limitations: docs/bass-temporal-v15.md.
Width-48 temporal convolutional KEEP classifier on actual post-V14 bass
intervals; strict .005 rejection threshold with frozen V14 acoustic guardian
at .3. This release retains all five earlier bass stages. The underlying
Basic Pitch, Demucs, vocal and solo-piano networks are unchanged.

Training data (CC BY 4.0):
GuitarSet 1.1.0: Qingyang Xi, Rachel M. Bittner, Johan Pauwels, Xuzhou Ye,
and Juan P. Bello. https://zenodo.org/records/3371780
BabySlakh v2: Ethan Manilow, Gordon Wichern, Prem Seetharaman, Jonathan Le Roux.
Cutting Music Source Separation Some Slakh, WASPAA 2019.
https://zenodo.org/records/4603870

Additional data: original project-authored bass signals and note sequences.
Original MP3 and separated full-mixture Demucs variants retain their existing
source-group training/validation partition. No user recordings or scores used.
Frozen provenance: training/results/bass-temporal-cnn-v1 and associated
bass-v14-baseline-v1 / bass-temporal-sequences-v1 reports.

Scope: balanced Full-song bass, pitches 21–59. Both CNN and acoustic guardian
must reject independently. Threshold equality, context edges and other
registers are protected. Retained objects preserve pitch, attack, endpoint,
velocity and source part. Inputs are pitch-relative spectral and acoustic
evidence, with no reference labels or fitted-model probabilities. This is a
limited-corpus improvement, not an assurance of correct transcription for
arbitrary commercial songs.
