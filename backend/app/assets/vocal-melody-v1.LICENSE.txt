The checkpoint contains a new supervised melody decoder trained on human note
annotations, using frozen acoustic features. It does not contain the dataset
recordings or the upstream Basic Pitch/Demucs model weights. Those upstream
models retain their respective licenses.

Architecture, training, evaluation and limitations:
https://github.com/MalharBhide/AI-Sheet-Music-Creator/blob/main/docs/melody-model-card.md
