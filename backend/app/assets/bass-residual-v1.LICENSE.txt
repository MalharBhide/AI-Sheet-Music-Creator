Training recipe and limitations: docs/bass-residual-v12.md.
Two 200-tree classifiers on actual post-V11 bass intervals, with fixed
74/28-feature context/guardian heads and strict .05/.1 rejection thresholds.
This release retains V10 note verification and V11 key-release protection.
The underlying Basic Pitch, Demucs, vocal and solo-piano networks are unchanged.

Training data (CC BY 4.0):
GuitarSet 1.1.0: Qingyang Xi, Rachel M. Bittner, Johan Pauwels, Xuzhou Ye,
and Juan P. Bello. https://zenodo.org/records/3371780
BabySlakh v2: Ethan Manilow, Gordon Wichern, Prem Seetharaman, Jonathan Le Roux.
Cutting Music Source Separation Some Slakh, WASPAA 2019.
https://zenodo.org/records/4603870

Additional data: original project-authored bass signals and note sequences.
Original MP3 variants and separated full-mixture Demucs bass crops keep their
source-group training/validation partition. No user recordings or scores used.
Frozen provenance: training/results/bass-residual-expanded-v3.

Scope: balanced Full-song bass, frequency-bounded Basic Pitch pitches 21–59.
Both heads must independently reject a note. Context edges, other registers,
threshold equality and either-head veto are protected. Retained objects keep
pitch, attack, end, velocity and source part. Candidate evidence is recomputed
after hold repair. This is a limited-corpus improvement, not an assurance of
correct transcription for arbitrary commercial songs.
