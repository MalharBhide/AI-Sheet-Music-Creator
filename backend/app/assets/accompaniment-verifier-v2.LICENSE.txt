notice is in training/results/accompaniment-verifier-v2/soundfont-copyright.txt.

Evaluation only: Oxford MIDItest, A. Sophia Koepke, Olivia Wiles, Yael Moses,
and Andrew Zisserman, Sight to sound (ICASSP 2020), CC BY 4.0.
https://www.robots.ox.ac.uk/~vgg/research/sighttosound/

This is a new event-classification network trained on frozen acoustic features.
It does not contain Basic Pitch or Demucs weights; those retain their licenses.
The final threshold was selected on validation data after two rejected attempts.
Prior tests are documented as consumed regression checks, not fresh evaluations.

Training methods, results and limitations: docs/accompaniment-verifier.md
