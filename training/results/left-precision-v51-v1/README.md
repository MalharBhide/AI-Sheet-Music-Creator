V48 frame head learns from physical CQT, attack and release windows and masked
reference targets, without distilling teacher frame predictions. Rejection-only
soft rehearsal remains a fitting regularizer on earlier fitting corpora.
No teacher output, corpus ID, source ID, absolute pitch or reference label is an
inference input. The V43 training-only normalizer remains frozen.

The fresh frame output starts at constant logit 4, vetoing every additional
deletion at both policies. Exact initial rejection parity and all-KEEP additional
permissions must pass on every complete training recording. Combined with the
immutable V43 output guard, initial accepted notes therefore match the baseline.
This is an initial decision proof, not equality of the deliberately fresh frame
probabilities or a post-training accuracy claim.

Two 100-epoch profiles retain V50's corresponding KEEP, presence, real-piano
emphasis and rehearsal weights. The rejection head learns at .00005 and the fresh
frame head at .0005; both cosine-decay to one tenth of their initial rates.

recordings retain every source partition and unknown-frame mask. PDMX is excluded.

Every earlier per-recording genuine-note, pitch, timing, repeat, chord, hold and
unsupported-duration preservation gate remains. Require positive overall,
real-piano, NRG-CP and isolated-pitch gains at both policies. One frozen winner
must then pass all 603 consumed regressions, one untouched 40-clip first pass
