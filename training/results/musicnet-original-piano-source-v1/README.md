# Verified original MusicNet piano subset

Credit John Thickstun, Zaid Harchaoui and Sham M. Kakade, University of Washington.
Original publisher: https://zenodo.org/records/5120004 (CC BY 4.0).

The complete original 11,097,394,998-byte gzip archive passed the publisher MD5
844764911fa0d5b97c97da944a057590, gzip/footer integrity and an independent SHA256
f26e6d023b7a62cd54d8f2b0533b88f549c30e3fad0aa546d1ea24aefba28fca.
No full archive was stored. Original metadata and per-recording attribution, the
complete source witness and predetermined work split are archived here. Audio
and label files remain local and excluded from Git.

All 32 selected recordings were found in the original training members: 21 works
for fitting, 5 for validation and 6 reserved, each with three 20-second excerpts
on original 44100-Hz sample clocks. No selected test-only recording was replaced.
The new MP3 observer is now preparing only the 78 fitting/validation excerpts
through the released V32 route. All 18 reserved excerpts remain untouched.
This evidence establishes source integrity/provenance, not transcription accuracy
or a candidate release. The publisher estimates about 4% note-label error.
