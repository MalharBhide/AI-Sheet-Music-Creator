# V47 rhythmic sampled-piano removal study

Two fresh 100-epoch profiles add 144 licensed NRG-CP sampled-piano clips to all
prior fitting corpora: 2,077 training recordings, 381 timed validation recordings
and 237 pitch-only TinySOL validation recordings. All variations of each new
progression family stay together. See the separate NRG-CP preparation audit for
source licensing, known synthetic key clocks, damping and zero-prediction cases.
The shared sampled instrument is not instrument-held-out evidence; the existing
15 real-piano validation excerpts retain their independent gain requirement.

V43 normal/stricter rejections are immutable permissions, never learner inputs.
The unchanged V38 release pass sees complete context before deletion. Retained
pitch, onset, release and velocity remain identical. Validation must preserve
every recording's attacks, holds, reference identities, pitch coverage, fixed-pair
and repeated-note timing, chord spacing and unsupported duration. It also requires
positive overall, real-piano and new NRG-CP gains at both policies, with positive
held-out TinySOL wrong-pitch gain and no lost fundamental candidates.

Both profiles completed; none of the 144 settings met all requirements. All
candidates are withheld. Some settings preserved every measured recording and
removed additional false notes, including on the new chord corpus. Normal-policy
real-piano improvement reached two notes among preserving settings, but stricter
real-piano improvement remained zero. These maxima may come from different
settings and are not a release result. The immutable guard prevented every
restoration. No consumed or fresh tests were read and no user audio or saved
scores were processed. The approved website assets remain unchanged.

The archived source witnesses and manifest hashes reproduce the study contract.
Checkpoints and audio remain local. Preflight records the 1,051 passing backend/
training tests (six skips) and 27 targeted preparation checks. The new data audit
is synthetic labeling evidence, not a human timing or universal accuracy claim.
